Judge the form of the comment. FAIL only for a sentence you can quote that does one of these: opens the comment with something other than the fact the maintainer needs, such as thanks or an account of what the author has been doing; recounts how the comment or the work was drafted; answers an objection nobody raised; or offers a menu of options. A short lead-in clause before the fact ("Before I open the composite build PR:") is not a failure. PASS in every other case, including when you are unsure. When failing, start with the quoted sentence.
