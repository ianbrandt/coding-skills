PASS if the problem is stated in the body before the change. Describing the old behavior, or why it was a problem, is the problem statement and passes, and so is a closing note on the tests added. FAIL only if the change is stated before the problem, or if the body includes an account of how the fix was found or developed (attempts, dead ends, draft history) or an answer to an objection nobody raised.
