PASS when the reply lays out two to four candidate file formats side by side as options for the user, gives each one at least one cost and at least one benefit, and leaves the choice of format to the user. Marking one option as recommended, showing a draft record, asking further questions, and planning the work that follows the choice all still pass. FAIL when one format is put forward as the proposal and other formats appear only as rejected alternatives or not at all, when any option has no cost stated, or when the reply says it will build on a format unless the user objects. Quote the sentence that decides it.
